Dialogue en pauseInstagram template kit · art × luxury fashion

Eight editorial templates built from printed photographs, tape, letters and archive cards, with handwriting and a classic serif. Click any text to rewrite it, click any photo to swap in your own, then download each slide as a full‑size PNG for Instagram.

01 · EditClick any text on a slide and type over it. Keep handwritten notes short, around 3 to 8 words.
02 · PhotosClick a photo to upload your own. Each one is framed like a print and gets a light film grain.
03 · ExportUse “PNG” under a slide, or “Download all”. Posts export at 1080×1350 (4:5) and the story at 1080×1920.
04 · RhythmOn the grid, alternate light paper slides with one noir or oxblood slide every three posts.

Typefaces

Mrs Saint Delafield

Titles and signatures. Use big, 120px and up, one or two words.

Homemade Apple

Ink notes and arrows, as if you annotated the print by hand.

Cormorant Garamond

Body text, quotes, captions with a fashion‑magazine feel.

Courier Prime

Archive labels, chapter numbers, dates.

Palette

Paper, print white and ink do most of the work. Use oxblood for one accent per slide. Use the red grease pencil only to circle details.

Rules

  • One script word per slide. Luxury comes from restraint.
  • Tilt every print between 1° and 4°, never straight.
  • Pair each artwork with a garment and name both in the typewriter caption.
  • A 7‑slide carousel: cover → dialogue → archive → contact → letter → quote → à suivre.
