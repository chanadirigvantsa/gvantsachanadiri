# M110 Luxe Collection: launch visuals from one photo

This board follows the TIRTIR Matcha Dual Serum carousel: one product photo becomes a full launch, from the teaser all the way to PR gifting.

- `m110-launch-board.png`: the 4:5 carousel slide (1080×1350)
- `01-teaser-motion.jpg`: the teaser frame, made from the original M110 photo

Frames 2–5 need an image model that accepts a reference image (Midjourney `--cref`/`--sref`, Nano Banana, Firefly or Flux Kontext). Upload the original M110 product photo as the reference each time so the packaging, logo and gold caps stay accurate.

**Shared style line** (add to the end of every prompt):
> soft lilac and blush palette, polished gold accents, diffused daylight, gentle shadows, editorial luxury skincare campaign, minimal composition, high-end product photography, 4:5

**Tips**
- Generate 4 variations per frame and pick the one where the logo reads cleanly. Fix small logo errors in Photoshop by pasting the real logo from the product photo.
- Keep the lilac/blush/gold palette in every frame so the carousel feels like one campaign.
- Swap the placeholder cards on the board for the finished frames (same positions).
